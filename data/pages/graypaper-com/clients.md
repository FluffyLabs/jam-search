## Clients

The [Jam Implementer's Prize](/prize) encourages multiple client implementations to boost network resilience. The table on this page lists the publicly known clients in development.

As of now there are 45 clients listed across the [5 language sets](https://jam.web3.foundation/#languages):

_A_: 10, _B_: 16, _C_: 11, _D_: 8, _Z_: 0

The conformance to the performance requirements can be inspected in the [JAM Conformance Dashboard](https://paritytech.github.io/jam-conformance-dashboard/).

If you want to list your client or update your listing, please request the change [here](https://github.com/w3f-webops/graypaper-website/issues/new?assignees=&labels=&projects=&template=client-listing-request.md&title=Client+Listing%3A+XYZ). Once completed a milestone remember to [deliver your milestone](https://github.com/w3f/jam-milestone-delivery) to the Web3 Foundation to claim your prize.
