# ⚠️ Deviations: These upcoming GP features are already included.

* `grow_heap` host-call out-of-gas handling follows GP [#533](https://github.com/gavofyork/graypaper/pull/533): whenever the remaining gas cannot cover the call cost, the gas counter is drained to zero and the call exits out-of-gas. GP 0.8.0 as published instead leaves the counter untouched on the out-of-gas exit, and applies no gas check at all on the "no growth needed" path (allowing the counter to go negative).
* `grow_heap` address-space limit rounds the stack reservation up to the 64 KiB zone size in accordance with GP
  [#538](https://github.com/gavofyork/graypaper/pull/538). GP 0.8.0 as published rounds it up to the 4 KiB page size only.
* The `bless` host-call does not refuse a non-manager caller. GP 0.8.0 as published (GP [#519](https://github.com/gavofyork/graypaper/pull/519)) returns `HUH` in that case. The check is not applied because the upcoming
  GP [#558](https://github.com/gavofyork/graypaper/pull/558) allows non-manager privilege holders to hand over their role via `bless`; both changes will be applied together.
* The gas cost model counts the destination register of `cmov_iz`, `cmov_nz`, `cmov_iz_imm` and `cmov_nz_imm` as a source register, in accordance with GP [#559](https://github.com/gavofyork/graypaper/pull/559). GP 0.8.0 as
  published does not state this, so the gas of a basic block that contains a `cmov` can differ.
* Program blobs are limited to 2^25 octets of code and 2^24 jump table entries, in accordance with GP
  [#559](https://github.com/gavofyork/graypaper/pull/559). GP 0.8.0 as published has no such limits.
* A block is invalid if the refine loads, the bundle length or the authorizer gas used of a work-report in its guarantees extrinsic are more than the work-package limits, or if its segment count is not the sum of the digest
  export counts, in accordance with GP [#563](https://github.com/gavofyork/graypaper/pull/563). GP 0.8.0 as published has no such rules.
* A service balance saturates at 2^64 - 1 when the transfers that it receives in one accumulation take it above that value. GP 0.8.0 as published bounds each balance by 2^64 but not the sum of the incoming transfers, so it gives
  no balance for this case
