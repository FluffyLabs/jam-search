## Comment by @davxy

The "output" field given in the vectors (which is generated by the STF execution) is not recorded onchain, so their order does not matter (see [here](https://github.com/davxy/jam-test-vectors?tab=readme-ov-file#stf-output) for more details). What is important for consensus is the post-state.

In this particular scenario, the reporters are included because they will be utilized later by the "statistics" STF. I have chosen to organize the reporter keys in lexicographic order.
