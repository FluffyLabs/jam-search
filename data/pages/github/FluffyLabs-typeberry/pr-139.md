## Comment by @mateuszsikora

I am afraid of importing from ".". Usually `index.ts` is the main file that exports the public part of a module and `import.ts` depends on the other files. Here the direction of this graph is opposite and it can lead in the future to circular dependency problem that is difficult to find because the message is "something doesn't exist".

I would move `fromJson` and `runCodecTest` to `common.ts` and then `index.ts` should re-export things that you use in `cases.ts`


## Comment by @tomusdrw

I moved the stuff to `common.ts`, I don't see the benefit of re-exporting everything from `index.ts` though. Seems like it's just extra redundancy. I get the point for separate packages and some module isolation, but here I don't see a point. the `codec` folder is not a module, it's just a grouping, since there happens to be many codec-related tests. It could easily be done on the same level as rest of the tests just with `codec-` prefix making the `index.ts` completely out of place.
