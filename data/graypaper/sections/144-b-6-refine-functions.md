These assume some refine context pair $\tup{\mathbf{m}, \mathbf{e}} \in \tuple{\dictionary{\N}{\innerpvm}, \sequence{\segment}}$, which are both initially empty. Other than the gas-counter which is explicitly defined, elements of PVM state are each assumed to remain unchanged by the host-call unless explicitly specified. $$\begin{aligned}
  \gascounter' &\equiv \gascounter - g\\
  \tup{\execst', \registers', \memory'} &\equiv \begin{cases}
    \tup{\oog, \registers, \memory} &\when \gascounter < g\\
    \tup{\blacktriangleright, \registers, \memory} \text{ except as indicated below} &\otherwise
  \end{cases}\end{aligned}$$
