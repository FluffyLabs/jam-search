The serialization of state primarily involves placing all the various components of σ into a single mapping from 31-octet sequence state-keys to octet sequences of indefinite length. The state-key is constructed from a hash component and a chapter component, equivalent to either the index of a state component or, in the case of the inner dictionaries of δ, a service index.
