The disputes extrinsic, E D, may contain one or more verdicts v as a compilation of judgments coming from exactly two-thirds plus one of either the active validator set or the previous epoch’s validator set, i.e. the Ed25519 keys of κ or λ. Additionally, it may contain proofs of the misbehavior of one or more validators, either by guaranteeing a work-report found to be invalid (culprits, c), or by signing a judgment found to be contradiction to a work-report’s validity (faults, f). Both are considered a kind of offense.
