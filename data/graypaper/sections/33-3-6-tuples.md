Tuples are groups of values where each item may belong to a different set. They are denoted with parentheses, e.g. the tuple t of the naturals 3 and 5 is denoted t = (3, 5), and it exists in the set of natural pairs sometimes denoted N × N, but denoted in the present work as (N, N). We have frequent need to refer to a specific item within a tuple value and as such find it convenient to declare a name for each item. E.g. we may denote a tuple with two named natural components a and b as T = ⎧ ⎩ a ∈ N, b ∈ N ⎫ ⎭.
