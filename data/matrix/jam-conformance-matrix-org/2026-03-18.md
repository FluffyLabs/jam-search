It has been a while since I last shared a trace batch.

We have recently aligned our fuzzer implementation with the GP specification regarding the encoding of the attempt field in the Ticket within TicketsExtrinsic. Previously, this field was encoded as a fixed u8.
Under under normal conditions (i.e., ticket_id ∈ {0,1}) there is no difference... However, with mutations enabled, the value can increase enough to trigger a different encoding if the implementation follows the GP (i.e., compact encoding).

This batch primarily is to get aligned on this matters. Additional issues may of course surface on your side :-)

If there are no pushbacks in the next few days, I will merge this PR and notify the W3F auditors team to switch to the updated fuzzer version that includes this fix.
