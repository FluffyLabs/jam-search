**piet** (2026-01-27T12:41:00.000Z) [msgid:$FDi5cqBbjzG6e0ghRWogxBKNPXwnU7ojEq7H_4gLR5U]:
We are most likely to address this topic end of this week in addition to updated terms and conditions. We are planning to use GitHub and the PRs as a queue for processing the submissions. Oldest PRs will be checked first. We will then most likely put an upper bound to the evaluation process  once shortcomings have been identified and shared. If the team manages to rectify in time, they can be proceed with the fellowship interview and eventually be awarded the prize. If a team doesn't manage to rectify in time they'll need to resubmit and be put at the back of the queue. This should speed up the whole process as unfinished implementations can't block the queue for a prolonged period of time.
