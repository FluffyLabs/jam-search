Hey everyone! I want to make another small announcement about **JAR (JAM Axiomatic Reference)**.

Today I worked with Grey to implement a PVM recompiler. Grey is actually not happy about the PVM design itself, in particular, the sandboxing requirement. To build a fast PVM recompiler based on current spec, we would either need the specialized Linux sandbox (as is currently implemented in PolkaVM), or rely on SIGSEGV (which is called "generic-sandbox" in polkavm), and that's rather fragile, especially security-wise. Neither solution is satisfactory: https://github.com/bitarray/grey/blob/master/docs/pvm-recompiler-sandboxing.md

We're therefore thinking that the problem is rather the PVM design. This means that to get optimal results and better performance, we probably must pivot on the specification itself. So here's our plan: we want to make JAR an independent specification, probably with a slightly redesigned PVM with a new memory model. The goal, of course, is to try to be the fastest in terms of recompile + execution speed: https://github.com/bitarray/grey/blob/master/docs/pvm-redesign.md

More eyes would of course always be appreciated! And I think if you're not especially tied to JAM Prize but want something genuinely really fast, this may be of interest to you. I therefore invite you to join JAR's discussion room: #jar:matrix.org
