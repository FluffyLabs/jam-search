I propose a new ad-hoc protocol spec for fuzzing: fuzz.

tiny cannot resize the validator set (it has only 6 validators), and full is too slow for fuzz run. The proposed fuzz spec is full with 85 cores, 60-slot epochs and short D and L. In a short run it crosses many epoch changes, resizes the validator set (6 to 255) and ejects services.

The values are a first draft. Looks sensible to me, if you have different opinion please comment on the issue: https://github.com/davxy/jam-conformance/issues/215
