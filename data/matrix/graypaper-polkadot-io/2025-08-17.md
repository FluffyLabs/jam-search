In general the instruction counter should be retrievable every time there's an expectation that you might want to interrupt the execution and continue during another block.

**jan** (2025-08-17T01:05:00.000Z) [msgid:$d31Wo9glpZp_AE9PlY4YnrDsSrxTTvjn9g8MYvClRB8]:
However, we don't want the instruction counter to be inspectable when the program terminates abnormally (due to a trap/panic/etc.) or when it successfully finishes execution and quits, since that shouldn't be very useful on-chain, and not requiring this makes the virtual machine easier to implement (and it's one less place where different implementations might diverge).
