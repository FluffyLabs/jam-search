## Release: 0.8.0

GP v0.8.0 is tagged and released.

The main changes here are:
- support for dynamically changing the size of active validators
- gas cost model

There are several smaller changes also, primarily for convenience as we get closer to creating useful services and over soundness as we get closer to audit.

v0.8.1 will see additional changes to the PVM and especially its paging model. Unlike the slog getting to 0.8.0, we hope to ensure 0.8 a short-lived series and get to 0.9.0 - and audit - much quicker.
