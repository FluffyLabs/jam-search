**gav** (2024-09-09T05:50:00.000Z) [msgid:$D5LTKgAWA7d-KQCUIGeLrUvezSpB5l2Nf7N4TRZwCac]:
The ceil(log_2(|v|)) just gives the (generally maximum, but here constant) number of nodes from root to leaf. Substracting x would result in a negative number in the case that there are fewer than 2^x leaves. So we clamp it to zero and take only the first such proof items.
