**gav** (2024-11-24T16:24:00.000Z) [msgid:$OSyv9eDTL_FzSTgTGBUga4K7Jrn8wd37d1OE3cHVtWY]:
In summary, anchor is used to anchor the WP (and thus WR) to a particular block which must (still) be in recent history when reported and ensures that the WP is recent. Lookup-anchor need not be very recent at all (it can be quite old), but must be finalised; this is used to ensure that the point at which the historical_lookup (in refine) is both finalised and within the amount of lookup-history which we maintain on-chain.
