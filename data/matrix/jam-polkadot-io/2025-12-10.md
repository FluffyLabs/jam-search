Re compiling something new, it depends if you mean as a JAM service or for CoreVM.

For CoreVM you can use https://github.com/paritytech/polkaports, I believe this is how Quake is built although for historical reasons DOOM works a bit differently.

If you want to make your own JAM service, the jam-pvm-common crate (https://docs.rs/jam-pvm-common/0.1.26/jam_pvm_common/) is the main crate to look at for Parity's SDK. You can build a PVM blob using jam-pvm-build (https://crates.io/crates/jam-pvm-build).

You don't need to use Parity's SDK though, other teams have their own SDKs and you can of course make your own :D
