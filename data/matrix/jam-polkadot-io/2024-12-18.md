CorePlay idea is based around actors whose memory exist within DA, but are committed to on-chain. Async and sync interaction patterns are possible via message queuing and typical async programming techniques, as well as co-scheduling. To the outside world (like developers and blockchain inspectors) it’ll look a lot like a regular smart contract environment except that the contracts are actually actors and execute perpetually. I’m not knowledgable about these of TBL’s musings but given his other work cannot imagine they’re especially relevant.
