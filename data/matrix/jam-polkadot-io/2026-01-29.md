**jan** (2026-01-29T12:47:00.000Z) [msgid:$lyyRhn9T-Rq1tA3FStEn5CRH9WLuibHjSbByGNRtZJ0]:
I will be adding an aarch64 backend to PolkaVM once I'm done with all of the higher priority work (which will take quite some time). That said, the current official reference hardware for JAM is the Toaster, so the expectation is that you will need to have an amd64 recompiler, and any aarch64 recompiler you might have will not be evaluated in any way. Depending on how it's written and on what hardware you're running it may or may not be conformant with JAM's gas cost model.
