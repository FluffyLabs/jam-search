**jeff** (2026-03-02T13:38:00.000Z) [msgid:$26MsSyziby0xus_BNiAQzC-AhPrETU_-uh-0UYipB5c]:
That's a bizarre design.  And I think davide's substrate implementation does not work that way.

The beginning any end of the epoch are extra sensitive for the randomness of future epochs, so if we have only a few sassafras blocks we put them there.

And we always put the "best" rVRF outputs at the beginning and end of the epoch. 

That's the reason why we do the weird outside-in sorting.  Actually there are two equally correct ways to sort, but they could both be described as outside-in in different ways, but they have different notions of "best" rVRF outputs:  (1) small is good and pull out ever other block for the opposite end of the list.  (2) both big and small are good, so you sort the list normally, but then trim or pad the middle as required.

In either case, the "worst" rVRF outputs land in the middle, so if you run out before you fill the epoch, then you just shove round robin assignments into the middle.
