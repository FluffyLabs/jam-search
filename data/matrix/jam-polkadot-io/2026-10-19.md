**luke_jamixir** (2026-10-19T06:59:00.000Z) [msgid:$hby5aVlWoMusiahMHO1OsQSnq86Qg03c9eqEo9DX8tI]:
I made jamboree, a tool for running multi-node and mixed-client networks, which may help other teams with M2. One command starts a local network of any JAM client, or several clients together. Every node's logs show side by side in one terminal and are saved to disk, and you can search a block hash across all of them. You can stop or restart nodes, split the network, or add delay and packet loss, without changing your client. Chaos mode does this at random from a seed, so a run that breaks can be replayed exactly. In CI it runs unattended and checks that all nodes end on the same head.

https://github.com/jamixir/jamboree

If it doesn't work with your client, tell me here or open an issue.
