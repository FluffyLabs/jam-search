**gav** (2025-07-01T10:19:00.000Z) [msgid:$Rot8lurT-vg5pirLYtPgXwql2C-lEIQDczFi4jB7PcI]:
But anyway, the root of the problem is simple: we are forced to fit more than 31 bytes of information into just 31 bytes of entropy. We need to split it opaquely by service since services are mutually trustless and we don't want one service artificially lengthening another service's keys' merkle paths. Services are 4 bytes, so that only leaves 27 bytes of entropy. This is now at the limit of security; any further opaque data in the key will reduce the effective entropy by which we shield permissionless data (such as lookups) potentially allowing a determined attacker to cause a collision.

**gav** (2025-07-01T10:20:00.000Z) [msgid:$PTyhmcru7yZpJ-rJLHe8laALi0Yj_22TmsnpYtAt3lk]:
