This would require implementing all system calls that LXC uses, in particular process creation and networking. This would take some time, but otherwise I don't see much of a problem to do that. The only remaining question is "Why?" 😃 LXC is basically a chroot with namespace-based isolation for processes, network, root file system etc. PolkaVM gives much higher degree of isolation and also full reproducibility. If your goal is to protect the program and the outside world from each other, you can just run it on CoreVM without LXC.
