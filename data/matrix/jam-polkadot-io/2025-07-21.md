What’s the plan for JAM and Kusama?

With Referendum 682, Polkadot governance signaled support for a future where the Polkadot relay chain is replaced by JAM. (https://polkadot.polkassembly.io/referenda/682)

In the JAM Implementers’ Prize, milestone 3 refers to achieving “Kusama performance”—which, if memory serves, has informally been described as around 50% of Polkadot’s performance (though I’m not sure who first coined that or where it was stated).

However, as far as I can tell, there’s been no explicit discussion—let alone a referendum—about what happens to the Kusama relay chain with regard to JAM. Unlike Polkadot, Kusama has not had a 682-style governance decision to define its path in a JAM future.

Of course, this ultimately falls to Kusama OpenGov to decide, nevertheless what are the possible paths forward for Kusama?

1. A fully sovereign Kusama/JAM chain with its own validator set. 
2. Kusama has a separate ParachainsService (or similar) on Polkadot/JAM (inspired by Oliver’s post: https://x.com/olivertaleyazdi/status/1947043501268455559?s=46&t=ThX7Y87rr1MIKyk6af4OXg)
3. Some eventual hybrid arrangement as part of the JAM Grid
4. Or something wild—beyond what I can currently imagine

I’d love to hear ideas. 💡
