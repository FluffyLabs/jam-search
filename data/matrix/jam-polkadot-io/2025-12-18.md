As per the prize rules, M1 conformant implementations get to up-rank a team-member to rank III, disregarding the usual time requirements. It does not alter the other requirements of members; the individual claiming the up-rank will be expected to be an expert in their implementation and how it relates to the spec. The rank III defence is always done in person and the individual will be challenged to demonstrate knowledge, understanding, personal development and ethos in line with the Fellowship Manifesto. In general, I would expect that the team nominate the individual who is most knowledgable about both the Graypaper and their code. M1 conformance does not guarantee a rank, only the right to skip the time requirement. If there is no single individual who can fulfil the relevant requirements then a team may be unable to claim the concession. I can’t imagine this would happen much, but it may be the case where a team has deconstructed agency to an assembly-line level and splits apart Graypaper comprehension, code architecture and code writing. I’d also expect to find it if a team overused LLMs to create their codebase.

The Fellowship induction/ process for rank II is generally not done in person and has substantially lesser requirements overall than for rank III. In terms of timing, I can’t speak for other high ranking members, but I’ll make myself available for the in-person defence as a priority once an M1 compliant implementer comes forward. 

Finally, note that the Fellowship is a collective of humans. Teams, per se, have no representation in it.
