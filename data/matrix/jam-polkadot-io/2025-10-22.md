**gav** (2025-10-22T13:52:00.000Z) [msgid:$zNaKIfQuXYJ8YfxUHjqeLV3_ITxuWxumomz50X_rTtc]:
It's not the actual speed of PVM which matters sadly. It's the gas model. And the gas model limits compute throughput to be ~10x-100x less than native/cloud. Furthermore JAM provides materially less interaction (disk I/O, network and GPU) than would be commonly expected of a cloud service. JAM cannot host typical software or languages in quite the same way (though CoreVM does lift this limit somewhat) but certainly Web3 services need to be designed from the ground up to use Web3 infrastructure and agentic, economically-sound design.

Both Polkadot and JAM deliver resilience together with scale (which can also be stated as low-cost).
