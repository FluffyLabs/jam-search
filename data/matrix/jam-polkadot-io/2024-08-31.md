While it is triggered by work reports becoming available, which is reported on-chain, the chain doesn't really wait for auditing to finish as such. If, as a result of the auditing process, a block author discovers that some other validator has misbehaved, then they will include this in ED. They may also build on an older block, if accumulation of some bad work reports needs to be reverted
