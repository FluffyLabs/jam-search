**jan** (2026-06-10T18:27:00.000Z) [msgid:$9K-gt6PQN1GZw-n65kcZYAXxuQ-XdavEDh5SiL5HH3o]:
FWIW, considering the (at this point) clear usefulness of such tools I'd expect everyone's implementations to be audited by AI at around the last milestone by a third-party (so there's little-to-no possibility for contamination), however at this point doing it yourself might be iffy, as it could be tricky to make sure the model doesn't inadvertently push you into a certain direction.

For example, any divergence from the GP could be reasonably construed as a security issue as it would at very least allow for denial of service attacks if nothing else, so in that case if the model points out that "hey, you're not compliant with the GP here" then that would, as far as I understand, violate the rules insofar as the requirement to have an AI-unassisted clean room implementation go.

(Remember that the point of the JAM prize isn't only to get functional JAM implementations, but to also expand JAM expertise around the community, _and_ to make sure the GP is solid, hence letting AI do the work here in any capacity is at odds with these objectives.)

(Also, disclaimer: this is just my personal opinion and is not our official position as it pertains to the JAM prize.)
