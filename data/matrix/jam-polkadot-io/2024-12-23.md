**davxy** (2024-12-23T14:57:00.000Z) [msgid:$NqCUWOUtXM9E8V3Gor7fSIvLJTmo6TFsw6lW2hz-0pU]:
copy/paste of an old reply given in the GP chan some time ago:

The public key for the scheme consists of two public elements, each corresponding to one of the two groups used by BLS for pairing: G1 and G2. Specifically, it is represented as (g1  sk, g2  sk), where g1 is the generator of G1 and g2 is the generator of G2. Each point in G1 is compressed into 48 bytes, while each point in G2 is compressed into 96 bytes. This scheme allows both fast verification of aggregate signatures and (pairing less) single signature verification. The full scheme is detailed in this paper: https://eprint.iacr.org/2022/1611 and is implemented by this library: https://github.com/w3f/bls.
