**gav** (2024-11-08T16:19:00.000Z) [msgid:$lUebWgl_yDdqItrLktW72Fyh6-5xYYxR-U8GxpEX1YI]:
Firstly, PVM is already pretty close in ISA to x86_64, by design, so x86 code should not be too hard to express as PVM. The harder part is making the I/O work - CPUs enjoy a much greater memory/disk random access bandwidth than blockchains due to the need to ferry data and prove correctness to all nodes involved in verifying. Secondly, the really hard part for a trustless ISA is gas accounting. A lot more becomes possible with effective secure deterministic metering and hyperlight doesn’t make any efforts to support this afaict.
