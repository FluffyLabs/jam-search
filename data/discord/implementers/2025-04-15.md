It stopped making sense to hash ALL the memory after EVERY instruction if there are like 100K steps.   In the end, Gav anticipates a "structured log" which will get the essence of the state of the machine.  I believe most host function bugs errors are found by looking at the registers alone and this structured log will cover it, but no idea.  I believe we should develop our own ideas of structured logs in the mean time.   I assume its very close to this https://go.dev/blog/slog but you can use this to output "traceBlock" and "auditWorkPackage" for any state_transition and refine execution, and it won't be much different in shape than what we already do with jip-1 -- probably just pc, opcode, regs for pvm execution gets 99% of problems solved...
